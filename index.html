Privacy Policy — Godsfall: Olympus

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Godsfall: Olympus is developed by ChabibiGames. This policy explains what the game does and does not collect.

What we collect

The game itself collects nothing. There is no account, no login and no registration. Your progress, settings and statistics are stored only as files on your own device, and are deleted when you uninstall the game. We operate no servers and receive no personal information from you.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows optional rewarded advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve those ads, AdMob may access your device's Advertising ID along with basic device and network information. That data is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by us, and we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

The game does not request location permission and cannot read your device's location.

Where required by law, the game shows a consent prompt before any personalised advertising, and your choice is respected. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time under Settings › Privacy › Ads on your device. Buying Remove Ads stops the game requesting advertisements altogether.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details. Google tells us only that a purchase was made, so the game can unlock the feature.

Children

This game is intended for players aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Your choices

Because the game holds no account and stores nothing off your device, there is no personal data for us to export or delete. Uninstalling the game removes everything it saved. For anything held by Google as the advertising provider, use the Google privacy controls linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top.
